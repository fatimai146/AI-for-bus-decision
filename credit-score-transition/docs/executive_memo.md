# Executive Memo: Moving Mortgage Underwriting Beyond Classic FICO

**To:** Credit Risk & Mortgage Strategy leadership
**From:** Iman Fatima
**Re:** Expected impact of switching from Classic FICO to FICO 10T or VantageScore 4.0

---

### The decision

Regulators now allow newer credit scores on mortgages sold to Freddie Mac and Fannie Mae. Lenders
need to decide which score to adopt, how to reset approval cutoffs, and where the impact lands.

### What we tested

We compared Classic FICO, FICO 10T and VantageScore 4.0 on **149,860 Freddie Mac mortgages
originated 2021–2023**, using Freddie Mac's newly published historical scores. "Default" means a
borrower fell 90+ days behind within two years (2,300 loans).

### What we found

1. **FICO 10T is the strongest score; VantageScore 4.0 is second; both beat Classic FICO.**
   In the riskiest-scored 10% of borrowers, FICO 10T finds 42% of all future defaults,
   vs. 40% for VantageScore 4.0 and 35% for Classic FICO.

2. **More approvals at the same risk.** For a lender declining its riskiest 10% today, switching
   to FICO 10T allows **350 more approvals per 10,000 applicants** at the same default rate
   (VantageScore 4.0: 260 more).

3. **Or lower losses at the same volume.** Keeping approvals flat, expected credit losses on the
   approved book fall by about **10%** with FICO 10T.

4. **The biggest gains are in the hardest segment.** For borrowers below 700, where most
   decline decisions happen, FICO 10T **nearly doubles** the risk-separation power of Classic FICO.

5. **Two credit bureaus are enough.** Two-bureau versions of the new scores performed as well
   as or better than three, which supports lower-cost credit reports for borrowers.

### How confident are we?

The results are statistically significant and hold across several stress tests. They hold for
2018–2020 loans, for early and later defaults, and for a stricter 60-day definition. Even among
borrowers Classic FICO rates almost identically, FICO 10T still separates good from bad payers.

**Limit:** we only observe loans that were approved. The findings apply to borrowers who
qualify for Freddie Mac loans today; they don't predict outcomes for applicants who were declined.

### Recommendations

| # | Action | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Prioritize FICO 10T in score-transition pilots and validation | Credit Risk |
| 2 | Re-derive cutoffs and pricing grids; don't reuse Classic FICO thresholds (score levels shift) | Pricing / Risk Strategy |
| 3 | Start with sub-700 and cash-out refinance segments, where the lift is largest | Product / Underwriting |
| 4 | Plan for two-bureau credit reports | Operations |
| 5 | Validate on our own application data, including declined applicants | Model Risk |
