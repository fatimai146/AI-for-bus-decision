# Memo: Costco FY2027 plan and FY2026 budget review

**Prepared from public filings as of Nov 2, 2026. Independent analysis; not affiliated with Costco.**

## Bottom line

- **FY2027 budget:** $322.2B in net sales (+8.4%) and $12.51B in operating income (+7.1%), with gas
  and FX held at FY2026 levels.
- **Watch gasoline:** at today's $4.31 a gallon, reported sales would run about $7.0B (2.2%) above
  budget, mostly in Q1 and Q2, with no operating-income benefit. Judge the quarters on underlying
  comps, not reported sales.
- **The real risk is underlying demand:** comps 2 points below plan would cost about $440M of
  operating income (−3.5%).

Underlying comps slowed from about 7.5% to 6.6%. Higher gasoline prices from March 2026 covered the
gap in sales. In profit, SG&A leverage covered a weaker gross margin and the end of the
membership-fee-increase uplift.

## Scenarios (FY2027 vs budget)

| Scenario | Net sales | Operating income |
|---|---|---|
| Spot gas and FX held all year | +2.2% | −0.1% |
| Gas +25% vs FY2026 | +2.4% | 0.0% |
| Gas −15% vs FY2026 | −1.4% | 0.0% |
| Underlying comps 2 points below plan | −1.8% | −3.5% |
| Comps −2 with spot gas and FX | +0.3% | −3.6% |

The last row matters most: sales can look on plan while profit is $460M short.

## How good is this method?

Run as a budget at the start of each year from FY2013 to FY2026, the method missed full-year sales
by an average of 2.6%. That beats repeating last year's growth (3.2%) and a Holt-Winters
statistical forecast (3.3%). Without gas and FX, the miss falls to 1.8%. Quarterly reforecasts bring
the sales error under 1% by midyear.

## Next step

Q1 FY2027 (12 weeks to Nov 22, 2026) is due in mid-December. Plan for Q1: $71.5B in net sales at
budget gas, or about $74.1B at spot gas, and $2.66B in operating income.
